# Lunar Reach assets

`earth_blue_marble.jpg`: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / Reto Stöckli,
Robert Simmon, MODIS teams, USGS and NOAA. NASA Blue Marble (2002), land,
ocean, sea ice and cloud composite, 2048 × 1024.

Source image: https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57735/land_ocean_ice_cloud_2048.jpg

Source and full credit: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-blue-marble-true-color-global-imagery-at-1km-resolution/

NASA imagery is generally not subject to copyright in the United States.
No NASA logos or endorsement are used.

All terrain, craters, rocks, platform components, equipment and materials are
original deterministic procedural geometry and shaders in
`stages/lunar_reach/lunar_stage.gd`. Earth apparent size and lunar geology are
art directed for the game's camera, not a surveyed astronomical simulation.

`crater_horizon.png`: original AI-generated lunar landscape matte created for
this game with OpenAI ImageGen. Its companion provenance JSON records the
generation prompt. It supplies the distant geological detail; the foreground
terrain, equipment, lighting, platform surfaces and character shadows are 3D.

# Source motion

Quaternius Universal Animation Library, Standard (free) edition. Downloaded October 2, 2026 from the author's name-your-price free download at https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library. Author overview: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html.

The original archive and extracted Standard files are retained under `quaternius/`. `License.txt` inside the archive identifies CC0 1.0 Universal. No paid Pro/Source content was downloaded and no supplied character was uploaded. Blender imports and retargets the animation locally; the source mannequin is not a playable character.

Use the in-place `Unreal-Godot/UAL1_Standard.glb` for motion and the `_RM` variant to measure travel where relevant. Per-character retargeting, contact cleanup and custom combat motion are editable in the character source files.

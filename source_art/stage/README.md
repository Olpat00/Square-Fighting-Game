# The Last Resort — editable arena source, architectural revision 3

`the_last_resort.blend` is generated by `tools/blender/stage_build.py` with Blender
5.2.0. It contains the actual beveled environment meshes, original material library,
independent service-lift platform, preview camera, and lighting rig. Decorative
architecture is joined by material to keep the runtime scene efficient. Rerunning
the source script regenerates the scene and both GLB assets deterministically.
`the_last_resort_modeling.blend` preserves the individual construction objects and
live bevel modifiers before runtime batching, for convenient component editing.

The rebuilt scene uses a stone-framed horizontal teak deck, concealed warm trim,
refined cantilever platforms, large-format terrace paving, an infinity pool with
overflow channel, drain grates and submerged entry steps, and a compact asymmetric
bar pavilion. Its bar has fluted timber, stone counters, glass shelves and small
bottle props, a slatted bronze canopy, four pendants, raised lettering and tableware.
The hotel has reflective bays, mullions, floor spandrels, bronze reveals and selected
lit rooms with curtains. Three curved palms have individual tapered leaflets; agaves and
ornamental grasses fill layered stone planters. Lounge furniture has slim frames,
separate cushions and seams. Six original anonymous guests form small rear groups.
Six earlier nearby tower studies remain hidden in the editable source and are
excluded from runtime export. The game's separately authored sky backdrop supplies
the city. The old billboard, overhead full-width
pergola, disc lamps and uniform stick-figure crowd row have been removed.

All geometry and the 21 tiled 1024×1024 PBR PNGs in `textures/` were authored for this game.
Textures provide original base color, roughness and normal detail for slate,
travertine, two teak variants, brushed brass, linen and palm bark. They are packed
into both `.blend` sources and embedded in the GLBs. Architectural UVs preserve
material scale across differently sized components. No external model or texture
source was used for these Blender stage assets.

The sixteen rear balustrade panes use their own `balustrade__Glass` batch and
`Glass | clear terrace balustrade` material: alpha 0.25, roughness 0.12 and
metallic 0.05, exported as glTF `BLEND`. Posts and frames remain solid; the podium
facade, pool weir and bar shelves retain their separate opaque glass material.

## Runtime coordinate contract

Blender `(X,Y,Z)` exports to Godot `(X,Z,-Y)`.

| Surface | Godot center | Width | Top |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main deck | X=0, Z=-0.65 | 18 | Y=0 |
| Left upper platform | X=-5, Z=-0.1 | 4.5 | Y=3.6 |
| Right upper platform | X=5, Z=-0.1 | 4.5 | Y=3.6 |
| Service lift | X=0, Z=-0.1 | 3.5 | local Y=0 |

Instantiate `stage.glb` at identity. Instantiate `center_platform.glb` at
`(0, 5.5, 0)` for the fixed arrangement, and move its root for the Party lift.
All combat stays on Godot Z=0. Upper-platform depth is 1.45 meters. Their visible
front edge is Z=0.65. The main deck front edge is Z=1.0.

The pyro vent centers are Godot `(-8,0,-0.46)` and `(8,0,-0.46)`.
The pool is centered at `(-1.35,-0.386,-6.35)` and is decorative. Rear terrace top
is Y=-0.28, visually recessed from the combat deck. No collisions are
exported; authoritative collision/support surfaces belong to StageController.

Group-prefixed mesh names `ReactionGlow__*`, `Water__*`, `Crowd__*`, `Vents__*`
and `signage__*` are retained for restrained runtime reactions. Stage material
emission provides readable fixtures in fallback renderers; engine lights and
sky provide fighter readability. Avoid making the pool or rear furniture into
additional walkable combat lanes.

Preview camera: orthographic width 25, equivalent Godot position `(0,13,28)`,
looking at `(0,1.5,-4.8)`. This is a review composition; the game camera may use
a shallower tilt and adaptive framing.

## Regeneration

```powershell
& 'D:\New folder\blender.exe' --background --python tools/blender/stage_build.py
```

Outputs are `Game File/assets/stage/stage.glb`, `center_platform.glb`, this
editable `.blend`, and `evidence/stage/stage_review.png` plus a geometry report.
The Blender review lighting and camera are saved only in the source `.blend`.
Append `-- --no-render` to regenerate editable sources and runtime GLBs without
the review render. The game owns its lighting, water shader, atmosphere and camera.

Revision 3 exports 379,996 triangles across 49 stage and five lift mesh objects,
with 30 runtime materials. Material batches apply modifiers by converting selected
meshes together before joining; the modeling file retains the individual editable
parts and their group metadata. The stage manifest records the unchanged combat
surface coordinates. The geometry count excludes fighters and the separately
exported breakable chairs.

The runtime uses a procedural dusk sky for coherent PBR reflections, dedicated
fighter fill/rim lights, warmer pavilion lights, timber clearcoat and anisotropic
filtering. Original shaders provide spectral pool ripples, caustics, subtle foliage
movement/backlighting, lit room interiors, floor warnings and tapered flames.
These replace materials and visual hazard meshes; gameplay surfaces, schedules,
damage and animation timing are unchanged. The distant city plate remains separate.
Three frozen 1080p art views are in
`evidence/visual_overhaul/scene_revision3/final/`. The architectural perspective is
a QA detail view; ordinary combat retains the side-on camera.

The matching breakable terrace chair is regenerated separately with
`tools/blender/stage_party_chair.py`. Its editable `party_chair.blend` exports to
`party_chair.glb`: ground origin Y=0, height 0.962m, width 0.621m, depth 0.720m,
facing Godot +Z at scale 1.0. It has 7,020 triangles in three material batches.
It shares the stage's teak, linen and brass maps and has no collision.

The inspection refinements in `stage_refine_surfaces.py`, `stage_open_pool.py`
`stage_refine_crowd.py` and `stage_refine_skyline.py` record the first export's corrections. Fresh
`stage_build.py` regeneration already includes them: timber caps stand above
their structural slabs, the pool opening has no occluding terrace substrate,
building bodies continue below the camera frame, and subdued guest profiles
stand behind rear glass or the bar. Nearby city studies stay excluded and hotel
floor reveals continue down the visible facade. All four finished gameplay tops
are preserved. `stage_generation.json` records source/runtime file hashes.

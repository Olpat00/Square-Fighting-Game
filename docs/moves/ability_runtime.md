# Ability execution and data parity

The eight kits use the values in `move_timing.json`, generated directly from the fighter definitions. A shared controller handles owned objects, confirmation sequences, counter responses, and visual props. All damage enters `CombatResolver`; props themselves have no gameplay collision.

- **Clutch:** Overtime freezes charge at projectile birth. Charge extends the data-defined lifetime and damage; Momentum adds a delayed ring at 35% damage. Personal Foul adds a 3-damage, 1.15-radius burst at the end of its rush. Second Wind multiplies the authored movement segments by 1.12 when empowered. Time Out uses its declared window and capped incoming-damage scaling; it does not spend Momentum because no empowered counter effect is authored.
- **Sunstruck:** the disc travels at 9.2 m/s, turns home after 32 ticks, and expires after 115. The same target may be hit again only after 24 ticks. A catch increments Encore; three catches empower the next disc to 1.45 damage and 1.15 radius. The single reflector has 9 durability, lasts 420 ticks, and redirects its owner's outward disc 30 degrees upward in the reflector's stored facing direction. The Main Event warns from action start and uses the exact 38–40, 46–48, and 54–56 active windows in its visible circle.
- **Heavy Duty:** command grabs confirm only one victim into the declared 25/45-tick captures. The controlled victim path visibly rises and falls before release. The ultimate creates an authored folding chair, removed after the sequence or interruption. Airborne Floor Inspection applies its declared downward velocity and emits an 11-damage, 1.65-radius landing wave only upon landing. The grounded version uses its ordinary 13-damage/radius data. Ground waves can be jumped and cannot strike someone on another height.
- **Deadfall:** rolling logs follow their actual supported platform, fall after leaving its edge, and can land on a lower top plane. A log's center rests one radius above the floor. Triplines arm after 30 ticks, expire after 540, have 7 durability, and trigger once. A third replaces the oldest of two. Quick Fix lasts at most 480 ticks and multiplies the next side/up special movement by 1.12. Branch Office follows its two authored velocity segments with limited steering. The tree warning and hit test share the diagonal capsule's center, 5.6 length, 1.3 width, and mirrored −48-degree angle; debris remains cosmetic.
- **Fine Print:** Point Taken grants a citation only between the declared 1.8–2.7 outer range. Citations expire after 420 ticks and cap at three per target. Side heavy and the ultimate finisher consume their stated bounded bonuses. Hostile Takeover's follow-up requires confirmed contact, occurs after nine ticks, and still misses if the target leaves its range. The ultimate's marked rectangle is its actual opener region, and the region closes once a capture confirms.
- **Rugged Explorer:** Trail Line fires one short-lived hook. A damaging hook hit reels the rival toward the Explorer and grants a 240-tick Route charge; shielded hooks grant nothing. The next side or up special spends Route for 12% longer travel. Anchor Stance has one-hit light armor. Uncharted Finish begins a captured sequence only after its visible lunge connects.
- **Urban Sentinel:** A timed parry grants one Guard charge for 300 ticks. Fortify Pulse spends it for a 20% larger, 30% stronger close pulse. The windup absorbs one hit of at most 8 damage, while a grab or stronger strike interrupts it. Lockdown Protocol marks its rectangle from move start and deals damage only during its authored active window.
- **Midnight Gentleman:** A damaging aerial hit grants one Veil charge for 240 ticks. Shadow Step spends it to travel 3.5 metres instead of 2.7, after visible startup, then strikes from its new position. Vanishing Courtesy is a backward evasive step with no damage. Neither step travels beyond the arena bounds; Midnight Appointment confirms its sequence only on opener contact.

Captured ultimate sequences use the declared duration, follow-up damage, and finisher values. Clutch and Fine Print split their authored follow-up total into three contact beats before the finisher; Heavy Duty resolves one slam. The defender can escape the opener by ordinary defense. A third party can interrupt the attacker or victim, releasing capture immediately. No missed ultimate receives its confirmation sequence. Capture links preserve the hold; the final contact releases it and uses the ordinary launch solver.

Object hit registries are separate from fighters' current attack registries. A later action cannot change an existing object's charge, facing, owner, or damage. Destructible tools lose durability at most once per attacking instance and hit group, including projectile hits. Arming traps, cosmetic props, warning markers, reflectors, and debris cannot independently farm damage or meter. Owned effects clear on stock loss/reset. Sequences pause with participant hitstop; independent projectiles continue moving. Ultimate warning windows and attached-zone rehit intervals track the owner's combat move tick, keeping warning/contact timing aligned through hitstop. A frozen spotlight pulse cannot hit again just because its world-time cooldown would have elapsed.

## Rebuild movement and impact data

`MoveDefinition.air_control` opts attacks into horizontal aerial steering without turning the committed attack. Unspecified moves default to zero; normal aerials use 0.45 of normal air acceleration, down aerials 0.35, and neutral/side/up specials 0.20. Down specials remain zero. Per-move overrides remain authoritative. Authored movement segments and ability activation impulses/teleports take priority over steering. Top speeds, jump velocity, gravity, and the authoritative 60 Hz clock are unchanged.

Move-family defaults set distinct hitstop and hitstun for jabs, finishers, heavies, dash attacks, throws, specials, and ultimates. Per-fighter row overrides apply last. Neutral/up aerial landing lag is eight ticks, forward eleven, back thirteen, and down sixteen unless explicitly overridden. Free soft/hard landings release gameplay control after three/five ticks; longer visual settling is independent of that recovery. Animation bones never supply combat collision.

Fine Print's Final Ruling (`heavy_side`) received explicit contact-range tuning after both-facing animation review: its local hit center moved from `(1.45, 1.08)` to `(1.05, 1.25)`, bringing the unchanged 0.54-radius region inward 0.40 metres and upward 0.17 metres to cover the straight punch. The uncharged region's forward edge is now 1.59 metres rather than 1.99 metres; opponent hurt-capsule overlap still applies normally. Startup remains 20 ticks, active contact remains ticks 20–23, and recovery remains 31 ticks. Damage, charge scaling, impact timing, and capped citation bonuses are unchanged, preserving the heavy attack's commitment. This is an authored combat-data correction; rendered bones remain inspection evidence only.

Training derives combo damage from the resolver's actual post-scaling hit events, including staleness. A reset removes stale-move history, transient recovery state, hit registries, passive resources, and owned objects before tape replay. Input tapes remain ordinary snapshots and cannot skip startup, landing lag, or finite recovery restrictions. See `docs/controls.md` for F6/F7 recording ownership and replay controls.

Counter windows can fire only once per attack instance. The counter blocks a distant projectile but its damaging reprisal must actually reach the attacker; it does not become a full-stage retaliatory attack. Directional throws use the authored angle and one facing mirror, avoiding a double-reversed back throw.

Imported authored prop materials are retained. Ring radius is scaled to the authoritative hit radius. Warning boundaries do not shrink or move independently from their dangerous region. Placement and visual posing never add a new platform, change blast boundaries, or override fighter collision solving.

Validation reports distinguish pure data checks from actual world-step scenarios and graphical inspection. A parse pass alone is not a claim of full ability correctness.

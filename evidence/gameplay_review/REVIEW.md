## Findings

No glaring missing-asset, exploded-skeleton, or main-combat framing defect was found in these samples. The camera follows the action toward the right side, shows recoveries outside the deck, and accommodates a respawn above the stage. The stage, pool, sky/city, furniture, all four character models, and four portraits remain present. Fighter silhouettes and small hit effects remain legible. The HUD panels stay separate and readable as damage and stock information change. The sampled poses cover attacks, airborne states, recovery, close contact, and a return to the stage without detached limbs or an obvious broken bind pose.

Two limited readability issues are visible:

- Frame 014: Sunstruck's respawning body is fully visible, but the overhead P2 label is partially clipped by the top edge. The label is back within the frame in sample 015. A single sample cannot establish the clipping duration.
- Frames 009 and 014: overhead P1/P4 labels overlap during close exchanges. The distinct clothes, foot markers where visible, and separate HUD panels retain other identity cues. This is a minor crowding limitation, not a missing fighter.

The sequence supports the earlier finding that the overhaul is visible during actual four-player gameplay, including busy contact and offstage action. It does not establish that every animation transition is convincing.

## Scope limits

This was a review of ordered still samples, not continuous video playback or subjective audio listening. At 2 Hz, brief clipping, rapid camera movement, frame pacing, animation blending, foot sliding, flicker, and sound timing may occur between samples. No claim is made about smoothness, subjective audio quality, the entire match, Fine Print (absent from this four-player sequence), or native input responsiveness. The capture owner's audio measurements and native/package verification remain separate evidence.

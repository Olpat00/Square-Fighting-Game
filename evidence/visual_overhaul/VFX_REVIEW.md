# Character and effects visual refresh — September 22

The canonical characters retain their original source people, meshes, faces, clothes and all 388 keyed runtime clips. Original metallic masks were restored, most visibly on Heavy Duty's bronze armor. The five new 768px studio portraits preserve the real face textures and hair; the actual roster selector and HUD crops were reviewed. Editable portrait studios and material reproduction scripts are documented in `docs/animation_pipeline.md`.

The new effect pool uses narrow tapered streaks, one thin expanding contour and soft smoke per normal impact; dark non-emissive tapered droplets are conditional on Blood. Jump/landing dust has a soft silhouette. Furniture fragments are ballistic wooden splinters/prisms with three-dimensional spin. All burst variants share the existing maximum of 120 reusable nodes and 0.45-second lifetime. Shader lifetime is an instance parameter, so particles share immutable mesh/material bundles rather than allocating a material every frame.

The shield now has a Fresnel contour and subtle latitude bands; its center is clear enough to see the fighter's face and clothes. Opposite facings were examined at both the actual gameplay camera and a closer same-angle QA camera. Ground markers are open arcs instead of small solid pucks. No animation selection, animation clock, shield size logic, hit timing, damage, or collision geometry changed.

`vfx_v2/` contains ten unedited real-game captures made through `presentation_scenarios.gd --case=vfx_material_review`: all four Blood/Flashes combinations at actual heavy impact and four ticks later, plus two views of both real held-input shields. All trigger checks passed. `vfx_material_review_blood1_flashes1_spread.png` shows distinct narrow sparks, a hollow impact ring and four small dark-red drops without a screen-covering flash. `vfx_material_review_blood1_flashes0_spread.png` visibly dims the energy while preserving those drops. The Blood-off counterpart removes the four drops and retains the energy. `flash_pixel_comparison.json` records actual RGB samples on two streaks and an unchanged shirt control, proving that the flash switch affects rendered output independently of Blood. This caught and corrected the first shader version's ineffective emission-only setting under unshaded rendering.

Impact positions now snapshot the collected melee or swept-object center for presentation. Low and overhead real contacts produce distinct effect heights, and a remote projectile's cue follows its actual sweep rather than the owner's move. The v2 frames exposed that Clutch's ordinary heavy had a broad contact center below its visible forward hand. A subsequent optional `MoveDefinition.impact_offset` now places that move's effect at local (1.05, 1.42), inside its existing 0.65m hit radius. The authoritative hit offset (1.05, 0.98), radius, damage and timing are unchanged. Other moves default to their collected center, and projectile effects always override with their actual sweep. The v2 screenshots precede this final cosmetic placement correction; the final full-suite frames establish its rendered placement. The scene architecture and lighting continued changing during these captures; these frames establish the effects, not final acceptance of the stage overhaul.
